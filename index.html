Important limitation

Android does not give ordinary apps a universal API to detect every screenshot or screen recording across the phone. This app can receive Android's screenshot callback for its own visible activity on supported Android versions. It cannot detect all screen recordings, screenshots taken in other apps, or external cameras. Reports are sent only if you configure your own Firebase project in app.js.
